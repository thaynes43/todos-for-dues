## 1. Scope

> **Ships:** the full happy-path job loop end-to-end for the launch chapter (Sigma Phi Omicron, UMass Lowell): Alumni posts a job → Moderator approves → Active enrolls → Alumni locks the date and roster → work happens → Alumni confirms attendees and marks payment-sent → either an Active or an Admin closes the loop. Plus the dispute path, the Admin view (with audit log + advanced settings), and Google Workspace OIDC SSO.
> **Audience:** members of Sigma Phi Omicron — Actives, Alumni, Moderators, and the chapter Admin(s).
> **Definition of done:** every PRD in §2 at status Accepted; every cross-cutting gate in §3 satisfied; functional QA passed against a deployed prototype on the haynes-ops cluster.

## 5. Rollout

- **Environment:** initially `*.haynesops.com` (Phase 1.1 — internal LAN cluster) per ADR-006, then `*.haynesnetwork.com` (Phase 1.2 — public via cloudflare-tunnel) once functional QA passes.
- **Audience cut:** Sigma Phi Omicron only. Single chapter instance. No marketing, no public landing page.
- **Reversibility:** instance can be torn down without external integrations to unwind (no payment processor — Venmo is off-app; no third-party data lock-in). Resend email sends and Workspace OIDC are the only external touches; both are idempotent and reversible.
